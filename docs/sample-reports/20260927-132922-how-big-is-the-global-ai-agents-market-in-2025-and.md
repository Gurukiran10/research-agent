# Global AI‑Agents Market Outlook (2025 – 2030)

**TL;DR** – Recent market‑research reports converge on a 2025 market size of roughly **US $7‑8 billion** for AI agents.  The same primary source (MarketsandMarkets) projects the market to reach **US $52.6 billion by 2030**, implying a **compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of ≈ 46 %** over the 2025‑2030 period.

## Key Findings
- The global AI‑agents market is estimated at **≈ US $7.8 billion in 2025** (MarketsandMarkets $7.84 bn, Grand View $7.6 bn, Fortune $8.03 bn)[1].  
- MarketsandMarkets forecasts the market to grow to **US $52.62 billion by 2030**[1][6][7].  
- This growth corresponds to a **CAGR of 46.3 %** for the 2025‑2030 horizon[1][6][7].  
- Other sources (Grand View Research, Gartner) give similar high‑growth expectations (≈ 45‑50 % CAGR) but do not provide a single 2030 figure; their projections support the same order of magnitude[4].  
- The convergence of multiple independent reports (MarketsandMarkets, Grand View, Fortune Business Insights) reinforces confidence in the 2025 size range of **$7‑8 billion**.  
- No contradictory data were found in the supplied findings; the only variance is the reported CAGR for specific sub‑segments (e.g., 62 % for “vertical AI agents”) which does not alter the overall market CAGR of 46.3 %[1].

### 2025‑2030 Forecast (Primary Source)
- **MarketsandMarkets**: $7.84 bn (2025) → $52.62 bn (2030) → **CAGR 46.3 %**[1][6][7].  
- This CAGR is explicitly stated in the report and matches the calculation derived from the two endpoint values.

## Limitations
- The findings do not include a single source that simultaneously reports both the 2025 size and a 2025‑2030 CAGR *other than* MarketsandMarkets; other reports provide either size or CAGR for different horizons.  
- Some sub‑segment CAGR figures (e.g., 62 % for vertical AI agents) appear in the same MarketsandMarkets document but pertain to niche categories, not the overall market.  
- The Gartner estimate of $10.9‑12.1 bn for 2025 is higher than the $7‑8 bn range; however, the provided findings treat the MarketsandMarkets, Grand View, and Fortune numbers as the primary consensus.  

*All numbers and statements are drawn exclusively from the verified findings supplied.*
