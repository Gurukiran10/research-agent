## Suggested Outreach Angle  

1. **Highlight AI/ML Talent Pipeline** – Emphasise the dedicated AI/ML curriculum and recent placement success (e.g., Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA).  
2. **Leverage Lab Partnerships** – Offer collaboration on AI research labs or industry‑driven projects to strengthen their AI ecosystem.  
3. **Recruitment Events** – Propose joint AI/ML hackathons or career fairs to attract top graduates and showcase your organization’s AI initiatives.  

## Limitations  

- Intake capacities are only available for BMSCE (360 seats) and PESU (≈6,500 overall); exact numbers for other colleges are not disclosed.  
- Placement statistics are aggregate; AI/ML‑specific package data is not broken down.  
- Some colleges (e.g., MIT Bengaluru) lack publicly listed placement contact details in the findings.  
- No verified growth rates or calculator results were provided, so no growth metrics are included.
